Our Offline Training Programs

Explore our specialized curriculum designed to take you from a hesitant speaker to an impactful communicator.

1. Spoken English (Beginner to Advanced)

Ideal for individuals who can understand English but freeze when speaking. We cover absolute sentence structuring, basic grammar clarifications, active daily debate practices, and real-life dialogue simulation.

⏳ Duration: 3 Months 👥 Batch Size: Max 15 students 🕒 Batches: Morning & Evening

2. Public Speaking & Oratory

Designed to crush stage fright. Students learn to frame speech structures, engage diverse audiences, master body gestures, and leverage vocal variety to command authority on stage.

⏳ Duration: 2 Months 🎤 Focus: Podium & Stage Practice 📅 Schedule: Weekends only

3. Pronunciation Correction

Neutralize your regional accents (MTI) and perfect standard English vowel & consonant sounds. This course uses intensive phonetic practice to ensure clarity in every word.

⏳ Duration: 1 Month 🗣️ Focus: Accent Neutralization ⚡ Format: Intensive Workshops

4. Personality Development

Grooming, mock corporate interviews, professional body language, and table etiquettes. Expand your self-esteem and build an impactful persona for the workplace.

⏳ Duration: 2 Months 💼 Focus: Mock Interviews & Dressing 👔 Ideal for: Job Seekers & Corporates
